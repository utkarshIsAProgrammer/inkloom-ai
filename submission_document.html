1. Executive Summary & Progress from Round 1

In Round 1, team indiedevs proposed Inkloom AI: an intelligent brand-governance co-pilot that resolves the core friction of decentralized enterprise communications—outdated product claims, inconsistent tone of voice, non-inclusive terminology, visual design divergence, and accessibility failures.

For Round 2 — The Build, we built a production-grade platform architected to scale to millions of users with zero-cost cloud sustainability, sub-5ms deterministic performance, and zero-knowledge privacy guarantees. The product features full multi-format scanning (text, markdown, images), explainable rule citations, one-click fixes, role-based reviews, verifiable clearance certificates, real-time free-tier usage metering, and command palette navigation.

2. Completed MVP Features in Round 2

1. Centralized Brand Rule Repository

Persistent storage with CRUD operations for Tone-of-Voice rules, verified Product Facts & SLAs, restricted vocabulary dictionaries (DEI & competitor rules), and approved visual palettes. Includes export/import and active rule toggles.

2. Multi-Format Real-Time Scanner

Dual-engine analysis for text copy, markdown files, and image assets. Evaluates formality, tone polarity, reading time, character offsets, WCAG 2.1 AA color contrast, and accessibility alt-text descriptive depth.

3. Explainable Flagging & Remediation

Pinpoints violations with exact line citations from the Brand Operations Manual, explains the legal/reputational brand risk, and offers context-preserving replacements with individual "Apply Fix" and "Fix All" actions.

4. Security & Zero-Knowledge Vault

Ephemeral Zero-Data Retention Mode (volatile in-memory scanning without disk writes), Automated Secret/PII Scrubber (AWS, API keys, emails), client-side WebCrypto SHA-256 signatures, and emergency data purge.

5. Sustainable Free Tier & Quota Meter

Live monthly usage tracker (10,000 words/mo included without credit card), interactive plan selector (Free Community, Pro Team $29/mo, Enterprise Scale), and zero-friction local-first processing.

6. Collaborative Review & Certification

Role-based access control (Creator Utkarsh, Brand Manager Shreya, Compliance Lead), document lifecycle tracking, visual diff mode, threaded comments, and signed Brand Clearance Certificates.

Hackathon Usability & Quick Access: Built-in "Judge Demo Presets" selector (SaaS Launch, Security Whitepaper, Social Campaign, Governed Showcase) and Cmd+K Command Palette allow evaluators to test all edge cases and remediation pipelines in under 60 seconds.

3. Complete End-to-End Product Workflow

  1. Brand Ingestion & Rule Setup: Brand managers configure tone dimensions, verified SLAs (e.g. 99.95% standard), restricted terminology, and approved hex palettes in the Brand Rule Repository.
  2. Content Drafting & Upload: Creators draft announcements or drop markdown/text files into the multi-channel editor (Marketing, Docs, Social, Press Release).
  3. Real-Time AI Governance Scan: The system scans content simultaneously across Tone, Facts, Terminology, and Clarity, computing a weighted 0–100% Brand Health score with letter grades in <5ms.
  4. Explainable Remediation: The copilot displays flagged snippets with rule citations, risk rationales, and one-click fixes. Users can trigger "Fix All" to automatically remediate all issues.
  5. Editorial Review & Formal Certification: Drafts move through role approvals with change tracking and threaded discussions. Upon approval, a signed Brand Governance Clearance Certificate is issued.
  6. Macro Analytics & Continuous Improvement: Aggregates department compliance trends, recurring violation categories, and the Guideline Clarity Index to identify training gaps.
